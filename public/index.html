        How the game works
        Playing together
        • 2–20 players per room, plus up to 30 spectators (anyone joining a running game watches).
        • Your seat is tied to a secret token in the browser. Refresh or lose connection and you return to your seat; a dropped seat is held for 30 seconds.
        • While you are away, your turns are played automatically.
        • On a phone, drag a card upwards to play it (a tap works too).
        Scoring

        Official UNO points: numbers face value, Skip / Reverse / Draw Two 20, Wild / Wild +4 50.

        • Winner scores: the round winner collects everyone's leftover cards; first to the target (default 500) wins.
        • Penalty (lowest wins): everyone else adds their own leftover cards; the game ends at the target and the lowest total wins.
        • Team mode: seats 1/3/5… against 2/4/6…, sharing one score (even player count, at least 4).
        Game styles
        • Classic: the usual rules.
        • Chaos: every house rule on (stacking incl. +4, jump-in, 7-0, forced play, challenge +4) with a 30 s turn timer.
        • No Mercy: 168-card deck with coloured +4, Wild +6, Wild +10, Wild Reverse +4, Skip Everyone, Discard All and Colour Roulette. Draws stack onto any equal or bigger draw card (in any colour), you draw until you can play, 7-0 is always on, and holding 25 cards knocks you out for the round. Leftover cards count as usual, 250 per knocked-out player; first to 1000. Team mode and penalty scoring are not available.
        No Mercy special cards
        • Coloured +4: the next player draws four unless they can stack an eligible draw card. It does not let you choose the colour; its printed colour becomes active.
        • Wild +6: choose the colour. The next player draws six, stackable with a draw card worth six or more.
        • Wild +10: choose the colour. The next player draws ten; only another +10 can pass it on.
        • Wild Reverse +4: choose the colour, reverse direction and give four cards to the player in the new direction. With two players the penalty comes back to you.
        • Skip Everyone: every other player is skipped, so you take another turn.
        • Discard All: discard every other card in your hand of this card's colour. The Discard All card stays on top.
        • Colour Roulette: the next player picks a colour, then draws until they get a card of that colour, and misses their turn.

        Stacking reminder: draw penalties stack on any draw card of equal or greater value, whatever its colour. If you cannot or do not want to stack, you draw the total.

        Draw and penalty rules
        • Stack draw cards (on by default): instead of drawing, the next player can add an eligible draw card and pass the total on. A +2 stacks on a +2. A Wild +4 only stacks when Allow +4 stacking is on. If you cannot or will not stack, you draw the total and lose your turn. With this off, penalties apply immediately (Challenge +4 can still defer a Wild +4).
        • Allow +4 stacking (off by default): lets a Wild +4 be added to a pending penalty. Needs Stack draw cards.
        • Stack Skip cards (on by default): a Skip can be played on another Skip, even of a different colour.
        Turn and hand rules
        • Call UNO (on by default): when you are down to one card you have three seconds to press UNO. After that anyone can catch you, and a caught player draws two cards.
        • Draw one card (on by default): after drawing because you cannot play, your turn ends. If off, you keep the turn and may play the drawn card if it fits, or pass.
        • Jump-in (off by default): play out of turn with an exact match of the top card (same colour and value). Not allowed while a draw penalty is pending or a colour is being chosen. Play continues from you.
        • 7-0 swap (off by default): a 7 swaps hands with a player you choose; a 0 passes everyone's hand to the next player in the direction of play. Does not trigger on your last card.
        • Forced play (off by default): if the card you draw is playable, you must play it immediately. This overrides Draw one card.
        • Challenge Wild +4 (off by default): the next player may challenge a Wild +4 that starts a new penalty. It is illegal if the player still held the previously active colour. If the challenge succeeds, the +4 player draws four. If it fails, the challenger draws six and misses their turn. Accepting instead means taking the penalty as normal.
        Turn timer

        Off by default. The host can set 20, 30, 60 or 90 seconds. If a connected player runs out of time, the game takes a card for them and ends their turn. Players who disconnect are played for automatically after a short grace period.
